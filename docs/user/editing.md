# Editing text, images and pages

Open the **Edit** tab, or **All tools → Edit a PDF**, for the tools on this page. Every edit is one undo step (⌘Z).

## Changing existing text

1. Choose **Edit text & images**.
2. Click a line of text. PdfCraft finds the paragraph it belongs to and opens it for editing.
3. Type. The paragraph rewraps inside its box as you go, and a preview shows the result as it will be saved.
4. Use the Format panel to change the font, size, colour, bold, italic and underline, alignment (left, centre, right, justified), line spacing, character spacing and horizontal scale. Paragraphs can become bulleted or numbered lists.
5. Choose **Apply and close** when you are done.

Drag a text box to move it, or drag its corners to resize it.

PdfCraft reuses the font that is already in the document when it has the characters you type. When it doesn't, it picks a similar font and tells you. Right-to-left text (such as Arabic and Hebrew) and Chinese, Japanese and Korean can be edited too, although some complex layouts may not reflow perfectly yet.

## Adding text

**Text** (**Add text**) adds new text to the page itself, not as a comment. Click where it should go, type, and click ✓ to finish. Each click starts a new box. Double-click text you added to edit it again.

Added text uses the standard fonts, which cover Western European languages. Other scripts can't be added this way yet.

## Images

- **Image** (**Add image…**) places a picture from a file. Drag it into place and resize it by its corners.
- With **Edit text & images**, click an image to **Replace Image…** (it keeps its position and size), **Crop Image**, **Rotate**, **Flip**, **Save Image As…** or **Delete** it.

## Any object

**Edit objects** selects anything on the page: text, images or vector drawings.

- Drag to move, drag a handle to resize, and change the colour of shapes.
- The arrow keys nudge the selection by one point; hold ⇧ to move it by ten.
- ⌘] brings it forward and ⌘[ sends it backward; add ⇧ to bring it to the front or send it to the back.
- Align and distribute the selection from the Edit panel or the right-click menu. One object aligns to the page; distributing needs three or more.
- Delete removes the selection.

## Links

**Link** (**Add or edit links**) draws link areas on the page. A link can open a web page, go to a page or a named place in the document, show or hide a form field, turn layers on or off, or run a script. Choose how the link looks (border, colour, highlight when clicked).

**Create links from URLs** finds web addresses written in the text and makes them clickable. **Remove all links** removes every link.

## Header and footer, watermark, background

- **Add header & footer…** puts text in the margins of every page: page numbers ("Page 3 of 10"), dates, or your own text, with its font, size, colour and margins. Choose all pages, a range, or only odd or even pages.
- **Add watermark…** places text, a picture or a page of another PDF over or under the content, with rotation, opacity, size and position, and whether it shows on screen, in print, or both.
- **Add background…** colours the pages or puts a picture behind them.

Each has **Update…** and **Remove** commands, so you can change or take away what you added later.

## Bates numbering

**Add Bates numbering…** stamps a running number (with a prefix and suffix you choose) on every page, as used for legal documents. It can number several files in sequence. **Remove Bates numbering** takes it off again.

## Page actions and document scripts

**Page actions…** sets what happens when a page opens or closes. **Document JavaScripts…** edits scripts stored in the document (see [Forms](forms.md)).
