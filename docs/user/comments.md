# Comments and review

Comments are notes and marks that sit on top of the page. They don't change the page itself, so they can be read, answered, hidden or removed later. Other PDF applications show them too.

## Adding comments

Open **All tools → Add comments**, then pick a tool. It appears in the quick bar beside the page.

- **Sticky note**: click where the note should go and type.
- **Highlight**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough** and **Squiggly underline**: drag across text.
- **Text box**: click and type text that shows on the page. A **callout** adds a text box with a pointer line.
- **Insert text** and **Replace text**: suggest an edit at a point or in place of selected text.
- **Draw freehand**, then **Erase drawings** to rub out parts of a stroke.
- **Line**, **Arrow**, **Rectangle**, **Oval**, **Polygon**, **Connected lines** and **Cloud**: drag, or click point by point for polygons and connected lines. Double-click or press Enter to finish.
- **Attach a file**: click to place a paperclip that holds a file.

After one comment the Select tool comes back. If you'd rather keep the tool, change that under Commenting in [Preferences](preferences.md).

## Changing how a comment looks

Select a comment to see its properties bar, where you can change its colour, opacity and line width (and line endings, for lines and arrows). Double-click a comment to edit its text, and press Delete to remove it. **Properties…** in its menu opens the full properties, including author and subject, and **Locked** stops it from being moved or changed. **Make Current Properties Default** makes the next comments of that kind look the same.

Your name is written on every comment you add. Set it under Identity or Commenting in [Preferences](preferences.md).

## Stamps

**All tools → Add a stamp** offers standard business stamps (such as Approved or Draft), sign-here stamps, and dynamic stamps that fill in your name and the date. **Create custom stamp…** makes a stamp from an image or a PDF page, and keeps it in a category you name.

## The Comments panel

Click **Comments** on the rail to see every comment in the document.

- Click a comment to go to it on the page.
- **Reply** to start or continue a conversation.
- **Set status** to mark a comment Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled or Completed. Its history of status changes is kept.
- **Mark with checkmark** to tick off comments you have dealt with.
- **Search comments**, **Sort by** (page, author, date, type or colour) and **Filter comments** (type, author, status, colour or checkmark) help with long reviews.
- **Copy text**, **Delete**, **Hide all comments** and **Open all pop-ups** / **Close all pop-ups** are in the panel's menu.

Pointing at a comment on the page shows its text.

## Sharing comments

- **Export all to data file…** saves every comment as an FDF or XFDF file, which is much smaller than the PDF. **Import comments…** brings comments from such a file into the document.
- **Create PDF comment summary…** makes a new PDF that lists the comments next to their pages.
- **Flatten comments** turns comments into part of the page. They can then no longer be edited as comments.

## Measuring

**All tools → Measure objects** measures distance, perimeter and area on drawings and plans.

- **Drawing scale** sets what one unit on the page stands for (for example, 1 cm = 2 m).
- **Snapping** lets points snap to lines, ends, midpoints and crossings.
- **Measurement information** shows the result as you draw.
- **Export measurements** saves every measurement as a CSV file for a spreadsheet.

Measurements are saved as comments, so other people can see them.
